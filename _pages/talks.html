Workshops and courses I’m teaching, and the talks, seminars and posters I have presented at conferences, workshops and academic institutions. Open a card for the event page, or use the buttons to download the materials.
{% comment %} Upcoming events come from _data/events.yml (_includes/upcoming-events.html). The year grid mixes the _talks collection and past events, newest first: each item becomes a sortable "date|kind|key" string, so talks and events interleave by date. Events that had not ended at build time are rendered hidden in their year; assets/js/events.js moves them over once they end. To add a talk, add a file to _talks/ with: type (Talk | Seminar | Poster | Talk + Poster), date, location, link, venue_short, logo, and optionally slidesurl / download_as / short_title.
