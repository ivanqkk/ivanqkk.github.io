### 🧬 About Me
I am an **Application Specialist in AI for Life Sciences** at **Stockholm University**, working within the Department of Biochemistry and Biophysics and based at **SciLifeLab** (Campus Solna) inside the Arne Elofsson Lab. 

As part of the **MIMER AI Factory** and in collaboration with **NAISS**, I help academic and industrial researchers scale and optimize advanced machine learning solutions on national high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructures. 

### 🎓 Background
* **Ph.D. Candidate** in *Computational Mathematics, Learning, and Data Science* (Joint program: University of Pavia & Università della Svizzera Italiana).
* **Research Focus:** Scientific Machine Learning (ML), Structural Bioinformatics, Molecular Dynamics (MD) deep learning integration, and high-throughput biomedical pipelines.
* **Education:** BSc and MSc in Mathematics from the University of Pavia, specializing in modeling and learning methods for life sciences.

---

### 🛠️ Core Expertise & Tech Stack
* **Methodologies:** Deep Learning for Structural Biology, Feature Engineering, Signal Processing (ECG), Biophysical Simulations, High-Performance Computing (HPC).
* **Languages & Tools:** Python, Linux/Bash, Git, `uv` package manager, Jupytext.
* **Core Libraries:** PyTorch, scikit-learn, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, RDKit.

---

### 📂 Key Projects
* **MOLECULE:** A dual-modal deep learning architecture for entropy-regularized classification and uncertainty-aware evaluation of kinase ligands ([JCTC 2025](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c01140)).
